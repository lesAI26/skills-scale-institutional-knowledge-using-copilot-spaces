# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers lead user-centered discovery and design, ensuring OctoAcme solutions are usable, accessible, and aligned with customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and translate findings into personas, journeys, workflows, and prototypes
- Define interaction patterns and accessibility requirements
- Collaborate with Product Managers and Developers on feasible, measurable solutions
- Participate in acceptance review and validate designs through usability testing

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and user satisfaction
- Translate customer needs into clear design requirements
- Reduce rework by validating directions early

### Typical Communication
- Discovery sessions and design critiques
- Prototypes and usability-testing findings
- Handoff notes and accessibility acceptance criteria

### Interactions with existing roles
- Work with the Product Manager to clarify customer problems and success criteria.
- Partner with Developers during implementation and with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility acceptance.
- Share findings and design risks with the Project Manager for planning and stakeholder communication.

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and operational execution across delivery, QA, support, and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the release calendar and coordinate deployment windows
- Verify completion of readiness criteria, approvals, and rollback plans
- Coordinate stakeholder and support communications
- Lead post-deployment verification and incident handoff

### Goals
- Increase release predictability and reduce deployment risk
- Ensure readiness decisions are based on a complete checklist
- Provide clear release status and operational handoffs

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews
- Stakeholder and support announcements
- Deployment status updates and rollback decisions

### Interactions with existing roles
- Coordinate milestones and dependencies with the Project Manager.
- Confirm technical readiness with Developers and QA/Testing.
- Partner with the Product Manager on release scope, customer impact, and communications.

---

## Data/Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data/Analytics Owners define, instrument, interpret, and communicate outcome and quality metrics.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into event and dashboard requirements
- Partner with teams to validate instrumentation and data quality
- Monitor launch and operational outcomes
- Recommend product, process, or risk actions based on evidence

### Goals
- Provide reliable evidence for prioritization and release decisions
- Ensure outcomes and operational signals are observable
- Turn findings into measurable improvements

### Typical Communication
- Metrics definitions and dashboard reviews
- Instrumentation requirements and data-quality reports
- Outcome updates for the team and stakeholders

### Interactions with existing roles
- Work with the Product Manager to measure customer and business outcomes.
- Give Developers and QA/Testing technical and acceptance guidance for instrumentation and monitoring.
- Provide the Project Manager and stakeholders with evidence of delivery and product impact.
